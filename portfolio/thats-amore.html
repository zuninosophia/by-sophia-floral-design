A CLOSER LOOK

The story
behind the flowers

For Ariana and Amedeo’s “That’s Amore” engagement celebration, the vision was a warm, intimate Italian-inspired evening, rich with romance and softly glowing candlelight. Sculptural arrangements of deep merlot reds, blush and cream blooms were accented with cascading grapes and natural greenery, capturing an old-world mercato d’amore feeling with a modern sensibility. Layers of pillar candles, votives and reflective glass brought warmth to the tablescape, while neutral linens, wooden chairs and textured chargers added understated European charm. A curated Italian market display, complete with artisanal treats and personalised details, welcomed guests into a celebration of love, abundance and togetherness.
