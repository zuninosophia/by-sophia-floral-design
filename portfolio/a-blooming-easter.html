A CLOSER LOOK

The story
behind the flowers

This Easter tablescape was designed to evoke a blooming spring garden. Low, garden-style vases were arranged so the flowers appeared to grow naturally across the table, bringing soft movement and an abundant, gathered feeling to each place setting. Cheerful yellow, lavender, blush and cream blooms flowed between specially ordered lilac taper candles, creating a lush and lively setting for family and friends to celebrate the season together.
